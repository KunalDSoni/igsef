### 1.2 `MR` versus `MH`

Older government CIN-format guidance uses `MH` as its Maharashtra example. This initially makes the supplied `MR` segment look unusual. However, secondary registry profiles viewed in August 2026—including this exact record and other mid-2026 entities—use `MR` for entities mapped to ROC Mumbai II.

Decision:

- Do not change `MR` to `MH`.
- Do not claim that either code is correct solely from pattern matching.
- Copy the CIN exactly from the Certificate of Incorporation/current MCA service.
- Record the source and verification date in the claims register.

Official MCA Find CIN service (may require CAPTCHA):  
<https://www.mca.gov.in/content/mca/global/en/mca/fo-llp-services/findCinFinalSingleCom.html>

### 3.3 Licence

Framer Community Terms:  
<https://www.framer.com/legal/community-terms>

As reviewed on 21 August 2026, section 7.3.1 provides a Limited Commercial License for free content. It allows use and modification in an end user’s own projects, including commercial projects, and client work provided the Free Content is incorporated into a broader end product. It restricts standalone resale, redistribution, sublicensing/sharing, reposting to competing repositories/marketplaces, competing template/component products, and claiming the free content as original work. The creator retains ownership, and the licence is revocable/terminates on breach.

Implementation decision:

- Use the template within this website end product.
- Do not redistribute the source as a standalone asset.
- Record the source and licence version/effective date.
- Replace demo content and verify rights for each photo, font, vector, icon, and code component rather than assuming the template listing proves every asset’s provenance.
